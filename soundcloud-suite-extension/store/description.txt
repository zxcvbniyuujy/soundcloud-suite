SoundCloud Suite adds the things the web player has always been missing: lyrics that stay in sync with the song, real audio processing, a shuffle that reaches your whole likes library, and a darker, quieter page. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

Lyrics that follow the song
Synced lyrics appear line by line for the track that is playing, locked to the audio clock so the highlight keeps pace even on sped-up or edited uploads. Six lyric sources are searched for you, and plain lyrics get an estimated timing you can correct with two taps. Translate lines in place, copy a quote with its timestamp, export to .lrc, or paste your own lyrics for tracks nobody has covered. A small floating bar shows the current line while the panel is closed.

Sound you can actually hear
A ten-band equalizer you drag on a live spectrum, with presets, per-track memory and automatic headroom so nothing clips. Enhance adds tone shaping, saturation, a harmonic exciter and a loudness-aware three-band compressor, level-matched so it never wins by simply being louder. Loudness normalization, Night mode, volume boost, a true-peak clip guard, speed from 0.5× to 2× with pitch that follows or holds, fades, reverb, stereo width, crossfeed, balance, mono and headphone correction. Every control is an exact passthrough when it is off, and holding Compare plays the original at the same level.

Shuffle your whole library
True random play across every track you have liked, not just the first page the site loads. Genre and artist filters, a blocklist, a memory of what you just heard, listening stats, optional scrobbling, a CSV export of your likes and a one-file backup of every setting.

A quieter page
Eleven dark themes and a light one. Hide the upsells, the sidebar, the footer and anything else you never look at, or switch on Zen mode and keep only the music. The player bar gains speed, an A–B loop, a sleep timer, a waveform timeline, timecodes, track info with artist links and queue export. A command palette (Ctrl+K) reaches every setting, and every feature has a keyboard shortcut; press ? inside the panel for the list.

Privacy
No account, no servers, no analytics. Settings, stats and the lyric cache stay in your browser. Lyric lookups go directly from your browser to the lyric providers without cookies, and lines are sent for translation only while Translate is on. The full privacy policy, source code and issue tracker are at https://github.com/zxcvbniyuujy/soundcloud-suite

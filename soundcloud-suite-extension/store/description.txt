Lyrics that stay in sync. Sound that finally hits. A shuffle that plays your whole library. And a SoundCloud that looks the way it should.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.


Lyrics

Open the hub and the words are right there, line by line, moving with the vocals. Sped-up upload? Edited version? It still lands on the beat. Press F and the screen is nothing but the lyrics.

• Seven lyric sources, searched for you — five of them with real timing
• The stage: full-screen lyrics on the blurred artwork, the cover beside them, a seek bar and transport, your choice of size and layout
• A text-only sheet follows the voice: the first line pins itself to the moment the vocal comes in
• Two taps fix the timing on anything still off
• Translate in place, copy a quote with its timestamp, export .lrc
• A floating lyrics window that stays on top of every app, and a small floating line while the hub is closed
• Share a lyric card: pick up to six lines, get an image for your story
• Remember three words? Find the song across every lyric sheet you have seen
• Loop one line until you have it
• Twenty languages to translate into, with a pronunciation line under Japanese, Korean or Cyrillic lyrics


Audio

Drag an equalizer curve on a live spectrum, or just switch on Enhance and hear the top end come back. Level-matched, so it never cheats by being louder.

• Loudness normalization, Night mode, volume boost
• A true-peak clip guard, so nothing distorts
• Speed from 0.5× to 2×, pitch that follows or holds
• Stereo width, crossfeed, headphone correction
• The tempo, measured from the audio itself, in BPM — and a lock that plays every track at the BPM you choose
• Save the whole Audio tab as a scene and recall it in one tap
• Every control is an exact passthrough when off. Hold Compare to hear the original.


Mixes and podcasts

A two-hour set is no longer one flat waveform.

• Chapters from the tracklist in the description, following the playhead
• Your own cue points on any track
• Long tracks remember where you stopped and offer to resume
• A Continue listening shelf for the sets you left halfway
• A long pause resumes a few seconds back, so you never lose the thread
• Every 12:34 in a description or comment jumps there
• Playlists show their track count, total length and when they would end


Shuffle

True random across every track you have ever liked, not the first page the site bothers to load.

• Filter by genre or artist
• Block what you are tired of
• Skip what you just heard
• Listening stats, a one-file backup, a likes export
• Listen later: a private shortlist that needs no account
• A private note on any track, a history browser, a playlist export
• A listening card: your hours, your peak hour, your week, as one image


Look

Eleven dark themes. A light one. Zen mode for when you want nothing but the music.

• Audio ads never play — the player goes straight to the track
• The suite in twelve languages, following your browser
• Hide the upsells, the sidebar, the footer, the noise
• Mute words, hide over-long mixes or tracks you already liked or played, straight from the feed
• Hide comment noise: emoji-only, promo and duplicates
• Open on the page you choose
• Title, artwork and seek on your media keys and the system now-playing panel
• Play, skip and like from any tab with Alt+Shift+P, N, B and L
• J and K walk any list, Enter plays
• Lose your audio output and playback pauses instead of switching to the speakers
• Speed, an A–B loop and a sleep timer in the player bar
• Track info, timecodes, a waveform timeline
• Ctrl+K opens a command palette for everything: type 12:34 to jump, 1.5x for the speed, or a title from your likes to play it. Press ? for every shortcut.


Privacy

No account. No servers. No analytics. Your settings and lyric cache stay on your machine. Lyric lookups go straight to the providers, without cookies. Translation runs only while you switch it on.
